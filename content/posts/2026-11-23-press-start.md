Welcome. This blog is a small TV wired to a controller. Every channel holds a different kind of writing, and when you open a post the screen goes quiet so you can read.

## How to get around

On a laptop, use the keyboard:

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move the cursor |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open (the A button) |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Go back (the B button) |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Change channel, or the next and previous post while reading |
| <kbd>O</kbd> | Options: text size, screen tint, sound, random post (the OPTIONS button) |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Main menu (the HOME button) |

With a mouse, turn the knobs on the TV: CHANNEL flips through the shelves, TINT changes the screen colour, and the speaker switches sound on and off.

On a phone, the controller sits under the TV. Tap the buttons, or just tap what you want to read. When you open a post, the screen fills the phone and the controller folds into a strip at the bottom. It hides while you scroll down and comes back when you scroll up or tap the text.
